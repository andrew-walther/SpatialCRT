## (d) Prose findings by section

where it was needed, and none removed the earlier copy. The main problems are (1) repeated caveats
(see the inventory's duplication list), (2) bookkeeping prose from the pipeline in the methods,
(3) a few 400-word paragraphs, (4) prose paragraphs that describe figures, which captions should
do, and (5) hedges stacked three deep.

**Sections 1–2 (simulation setting, designs).** They restate main-text Equation 1 and the regime
definitions nearly verbatim, and the Checkerboard history runs eight lines.
