## SHOULD

1. **Methods, application, "Primary queen settings…": "the six designs, the two variants and SRS".** The body never introduces the variants. They appear only in the Table 1 caption, so a reader asks "which variants?" *Rewrite (+2):* "the six designs, the two Table 1 variants and SRS".

2. **Continued work: "High Incidence Focus reaches a smaller share of the population".** Smaller than what? Results give population shares only for High Incidence Focus (22–24%) and Isolation Buffer (27%), never for SRS or Incidence-Guided Saturation, so the comparison has no reference. *Rewrite (+1):* "High Incidence Focus reaches only 22–24% of the working-age population".

3. **Limitations, second paragraph: "Worst-decile estimates depend on the sampled assignments and the finite number of outcomes per assignment."** This one-sentence paragraph reads as an afterthought, which is the choppiness the author flagged. *Rewrite (word-neutral):* join it to the end of the preceding paragraph: "…for changes between planning and implementation, and worst-decile estimates depend on the sampled assignments and the finite number of outcomes per assignment."

4. **Results, NC ¶3: "Spatial blocking was close to SRS under both-arms spillover and varied more across years under control-only spillover."** "Varied more" has no comparison, so the reader cannot tell more than what. *Rewrite (−1):* "…close to SRS under both-arms spillover but varied by year under control-only spillover."

## NIT

5. **Introduction ¶2: "maximal interspersion can make the two inseparable. In a strict checkerboard with edge neighbors…"** "Interspersion" is jargon for a clinician, and "edge neighbors" comes before contiguity is defined. *Rewrite (+1):* "fully alternating treatment can make the two inseparable. In a strict checkerboard where neighbors share an edge, for example…" (+3 for the second sentence. Use only the first change if the budget is tight.)

6. **Abstract Results: "On the grid with treatment effect τ = 1".** A clinician reading only the abstract cannot interpret 1. *Rewrite (abstract only, +5):* "τ = 1 (one standard deviation of outcome noise)", if the abstract limit allows.

7. **Results, power ¶: "Incidence-Guided Saturation's MSE advantage over SRS fell from 22% with 58 colleges".** Set beside the 21.1–29.9% headline, this reads like a third number for the same quantity. It comes from the power setting (ρ = 0.5, γ = 0.8τ). *Rewrite (+3):* "…advantage over SRS in this setting fell from 22%…". Optional within the word budget.

8. **Figure 3 caption.** The text cites Figure 3 for the worst-decile results (0.1912 vs 0.3462, and 20.7–43.4%), but the figure shows percentiles and means, not worst-decile means. *Add (captions are not counted):* "Worst-decile means are reported in the text."

**Verdict:** ready for author sign-off once SHOULD 1–4 are applied. They are wording fixes that change no numbers or claims.
