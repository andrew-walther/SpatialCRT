## 5. Sensitivity study of Incidence-Guided Saturation

Report: `docs/findings/2026-11-24-igs-sensitivity-findings.md` (method, results table, figure
`results/igs_sensitivity_2026-11/full/summary/fig_igs_re_vs_srs.png`). Reviewed independently
(`docs/reviews/2026-11-24-igs-sensitivity-review.md`); code correct, and the review's corrections
to the report are applied. The run reproduced the production Designs 3, 8 and 9 exactly.

- 30 variants (2, 4, 10 or 25 equal regions × spreads 60/40 to 90/10 × guided or unguided) on the
  full grid. Under control-only spillover, large regions and contrasting saturations beat SRS
  (two or four regions at 70/30 or wider: 16% to 45% lower MSE); 25 small blocks never did. Under
  both-arms spillover wider spreads cost accuracy. The original (four quadrants, 80/20) sits in
  between: 33% lower than SRS under control-only and 9% higher under both-arms spillover.
- Guidance itself changed MSE by up to 10% against the same design without it (always a precision
  cost when detectable) and moved more treatment to high-incidence regions (burden share up to
  0.58 against 0.50).
- Caveat for the committee: the number of regions moves together with the saturation contrast
  and region shape, so the study shows which combinations work, not which factor drives them.
- **Decisions: the spec's choices (regions, spreads, grid only, pooling over effect sizes);
  placement (SI section and figure, one optional CTJ sentence of about 35 words that fits the
  word budget, a Chapter 3 appendix mirror, a backup slide for Q-16).**
