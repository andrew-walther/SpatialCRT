# CTJ figure legends

Separate legend sheet from the shared exhibit definitions. Both layouts use the
same captions and vector graphics. Numbering follows first citation: NC map, grid
accuracy, grid decomposition, NC annual accuracy.

Figure 1. Observed North Carolina planning inputs. (A) Illustrative 2018 SUD rates per 100,000 population aged 18--64 for the 58 service-area clusters; all four years are evaluated separately. (B) The frozen four-region partition used by both saturation strategies in every year. Incidence guides primary allocation only; it does not enter the education-outcome baseline. All-year maps and assignment examples are in the supplementary material.

Figure 2. Grid mean MSE at $\tau=1$ with queen weights, for the detailed six-design subset. Points keep the five incidence configurations separate, averaging 16 outcome-dependence/spillover-magnitude settings within each configuration and regime. Parentheses in the legend give incidence dependence $\rho_X$. Both panels use the same logarithmic scale. Table 2 includes all nine designs. Parameter-specific displays remain in the supplementary material; no pooled Monte Carlo intervals are implied.

Figure 3. Composition of grid mean MSE for the six-design subset, Poisson incidence with $\rho_X=0.2$, $\tau=1$ and queen weights. Squared bias and empirical error variance are averaged over 16 settings separately in each spillover regime, then divided by mean MSE. Labels show the squared-bias percentage. Empirical variance uses $(249/250)\mathrm{SD}^2$, so its sum with squared bias equals reported MSE exactly. This figure describes one incidence configuration, not all grid settings.

Figure 4. Completed primary application: yearly mean MSE divided by year- and regime-matched SRS MSE, for all nine designs with queen weights and education outcomes. Each cell averages six $\rho/\gamma$ settings equally before taking the ratio. Blue indicates lower error than SRS, orange higher error; colors use a logarithmic ratio scale and cells print the ratios. SRS mean MSE is 0.07638 under both arms and 0.30761 control-only. Shared annual sources are not independent replications. Absolute MSE, bias, coverage, budgets and setting-specific comparisons are in the supplementary material.
