# CTJ figure legends

Separate legend sheet from the shared exhibit definitions. Both layouts use the
same captions and graphics. Numbering follows first citation: NC map, grid
accuracy, allocation-specific MSE distribution, NC annual accuracy.

Figure 1. Observed North Carolina sudden unexpected death (SUD) incidence. (A) SUD rates per 100,000 population aged 18--64 for the 58 service-area clusters, pooled over 2018--2021 (deaths divided by person-years). (B) The fixed four-region partition used by Incidence-Guided Saturation in every year. (C) Annual rates on the same color scale. Each year is analyzed separately, so the year-to-year shifts in panel C explain why some designs' results vary by year (Figure 4). Incidence guides treatment allocation only and does not enter the education outcome.

Figure 2. Grid mean MSE at $\tau=1$ with queen contiguity for the six designs and simple random sampling. Results are shown separately for the five incidence configurations, each averaging the 16 outcome-dependence and spillover-magnitude settings within its spillover regime. Parentheses in the legend give the incidence dependence $\rho_X$. Incidence configuration changes mean MSE little for every design except High Incidence Focus. Horizontal lines are 95\% Monte Carlo intervals over the ten simulated incidence surfaces and are often narrower than the symbols. Designs share surfaces and outcome noise, so Table 2 gives the paired comparison of each design with simple random sampling.

Figure 3. Distribution of assignment-specific MSE for the six designs and simple random sampling (SRS), relative to the mean MSE of SRS in the same setting (queen contiguity, $\tau=1$). Figure 2 and Table 2 report average MSE, whereas this figure shows how MSE varies from one assignment to another (allocation risk). Grid results use all 50 configuration-by-surface units at $\rho\in\{0,0.5\}$ and $\gamma\in\{0.5,0.8\}$, and application results use $\rho\in\{0,0.5\}$ and $\gamma=0.8$ in each year 2018--2021. In the application, $2\times2$ Blocking denotes its spatial-blocking adaptation. Thin lines span the 5th to 95th percentiles of assignments, thick bars the 25th to 75th, and circles mark the mean ratio. Percentiles and means are computed within each setting and then averaged across settings. Outcome noise from the finite number of outcomes per assignment widens the bars, and worst-decile means are reported in the text. A design with one possible assignment per setting appears as a circle only.

Figure 4. Application mean MSE by year, divided by the mean MSE of simple random sampling (SRS) in the same year and spillover regime, for the six designs with queen contiguity. Each cell averages six $\rho$ and $\gamma$ settings. Blue indicates lower error than SRS and orange higher error, on a logarithmic color scale. SRS mean MSE is 0.07638 under both arms and 0.30761 under control-only spillover. In the application, $2\times2$ Blocking denotes its spatial-blocking adaptation. Absolute MSE, bias and coverage are in the SI.
