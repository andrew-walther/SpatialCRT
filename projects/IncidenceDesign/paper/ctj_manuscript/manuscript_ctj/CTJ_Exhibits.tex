Each regime-specific entry averages 80 scenarios, from five incidence configurations, four values of the spatial dependence coefficient $\rho$ and four spillover-magnitude values $\psi$. MSE is the mean squared error of the estimate of $\beta$, and coverage is the proportion of 95\% $t$-based confidence intervals (CIs), with a degrees-of-freedom-corrected standard error, that contain $\beta$. Both use the spatial lag model that includes the true spillover covariate $\mathbf{S}(\mathbf{Z})$, with $\psi$ estimated. The ratio to SRS divides each design's MSE by that of simple random sampling (SRS), which randomly selects 50 of the 100 clusters for treatment. Its 95\% interval is one plus the paired design-minus-SRS interval for the mean difference in MSE across the 50 independent configuration-by-surface units, divided by the SRS MSE.
