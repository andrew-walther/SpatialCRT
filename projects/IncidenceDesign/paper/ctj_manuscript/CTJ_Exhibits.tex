\midrule

High Incidence Focus & Treat the 50 clusters with the highest incidence; ties are broken at random. & Treat the 29 clusters with the highest observed rate. \\
Isolation Buffer & Treat clusters one at a time, each chosen at random from the untreated clusters with no treated neighbor, until none remain; no two treated clusters are neighbors (about 21 treated). & Same rule on the NC adjacency graph (about 16 treated). \\
$2\times2$ Blocking & Divide the grid into 25 blocks of $2\times2$ clusters and treat two randomly chosen clusters in each block (50 treated). & Treat a random half (rounded) of each of 14 fixed groups formed by $k$-means clustering on location, incidence rank and population (27--31 treated by year). \\
Balanced Quartiles & Divide clusters into quartiles of incidence rank and treat a random half of each quartile (50 treated). & Same rule with observed rates (29 treated). \\
Incidence-Guided Saturation & Treat a random 80\%, 60\%, 40\% and 20\% of the clusters in the four $5\times5$ quadrants, ordered from highest to lowest mean incidence (50 treated). & Same rule with four fixed geographic regions, ordered by the mean rank of their clusters' observed rates (29 treated). \\
Simple Random Sampling & Treat a completely random half of the clusters (50 treated). & Same rule (29 treated). \\
