# presentation/

Talk deck for Project 1 (SpillSpatialDepSim), about 20 minutes: **Part I of the prelim
exam presentation** (Monday 2026-12-07) and the seed of Dr. Lin's conference talk in
December. It is a standalone deck. The steps that adapt it into the prelim deck in
`bios-dissertation/slides/prelim/part-1/` are at the end of this file. Copy it there
only after Andrew approves it.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `spillover_talk.qmd` | Deck source (Quarto revealjs, 1920×1080). The setup chunk computes every number on the slides and in the speaker notes from the saved results; nothing is typed by hand. Speaker notes hold the read-aloud script (one sentence per bullet) with `[time \| running total]`; backup slides have short "use when asked" notes. |
| `spillover_talk.html` | Rendered deck, self-contained (fonts, figures and MathJax embedded), about 7 MB. Open in a browser; **S** opens the speaker view (script, next slide, timer), **M** the slide menu, **F** full screen. |
| `spillover_talk.revealjs.md` | The knitted deck with the computed numbers filled in (`keep-md: true`). `tools/outline_from_deck.py` reads it. Regenerated by every render. |
| `OUTLINE.md` | Decisions, timing, trims for shorter conference slots, open questions, and one entry per slide (generated from the deck) with its both / prelim / conference tag. |
| `outline.html` | Visual version of `OUTLINE.md` with figures inline. |
| `theme/unc-gillings.css`, `theme/prelim-overrides.css`, `theme/footer-part.html` | Copies of the prelim template theme from `bios-dissertation/slides/prelim/test/`, unchanged (identical to Part II's copies). `footer-part.html` writes each slide's `data-footer-part` (the section name) into the footer. |
| `theme/deck.css` | Everything specific to this deck. Every class is prefixed `p1-`, so it cannot clash with Part II's `deck.css`. |
| `figures/` | PNGs used by the deck and outline. `fig_*.png` and `bss_rank_among_srs.csv` are made by `tools/make_figures.R`; the rest are the manuscript's own figures (3×3 and 3×4 in their corrected versions). |
| `tools/load_results.R` | Loads the per-allocation β̂ MSE results (`results/beta_mse/`, corrected reruns in `review/results/`) and the corrected summary tables (`review/tables/`), and stops if the two disagree. Used by the figures and the deck. |
| `tools/make_figures.R` | Slide-sized figures: every 2×4 allocation's MSE (BSS marked), 2×4 MSE by ψ for each spillover type, 3×3 and 3×4 means side by side, where BSS falls among random allocations on every grid, and the checkerboard collinearity diagram. No in-figure titles. |
| `tools/outline_from_deck.py` | Regenerates the slide entries of `OUTLINE.md` from the knitted deck; holds the carry-over tags and the prelim and conference notes. |
| `tools/build_outline_html.py` | Builds `outline.html` from `OUTLINE.md`. |

Reviews of the deck: `../review/deck-reviews/2026-10-28-part1-deck-*` (rounds 1–4, a statistician and a non-statistician reviewer each round).

## Build

From the project root (`SpillSpatialDepSim/`):

```bash
Rscript presentation/tools/make_figures.R
```

```bash
cd presentation && /Applications/RStudio.app/Contents/Resources/app/quarto/bin/quarto render spillover_talk.qmd
```

```bash
python3 presentation/tools/outline_from_deck.py && python3 presentation/tools/build_outline_html.py
```

Use RStudio's bundled Quarto; `/usr/local/bin/quarto` fails on this machine. The render
also writes `spillover_talk_files/`, which the self-contained HTML does not use
(git-ignored).

## Conventions

- Section names (Background, Methods, Simulation Results, Application, Discussion,
  Backup) appear in the footer; there are no section-divider slides.
- Slide titles are claims stated as sentences; figures carry no in-figure titles.
- Spillover types use the manuscript's names, Control Clusters Only and Control &
  Intervention Clusters.
- The corrected 3×3 and 3×4 results are presented as the results; the slides do not
  mention the correction.
- Numbers come from the setup chunk. To change a number, change the results or the
  computation, not the slide text.

## Adapting this deck for the prelim (Part I)

The prelim deck (`bios-dissertation/slides/prelim/`, skeleton in
`test/slide-deck-test/prelim-test.qmd`) has one title and one agenda, Parts I–III each
opening with its divider, one closing "Thank you" slide after Part III, then a Backup
divider and three backup sections, one per part. Copy this deck into
`bios-dissertation/slides/prelim/part-1/` only after Andrew approves it, then make these
changes (author, 2026-10-28):

1. **Cut the standalone frame.**
   - Drop the title slide and the Outline slide (tag **conference**). The prelim has its
     own title and agenda.
   - Drop the "Thank you!" slide. Its acknowledgements move to the single prelim
     closing slide after Part III, grouped with Parts II and III: Dr. Lin (advisor),
     Dr. Van Deinse (collaborator), National Institute of Mental Health K01MH129619
     (Dr. Van Deinse), and Bureau of Justice Assistance 15PBJA-24-GG-01896-PRJH awarded
     to the NC Department of Adult Correction.
   - The paper reference (Walther, Van Deinse and Lin, *BMC Medical Research
     Methodology*, 2026) is already on the last Discussion slide, "These results come
     from small regular grids and a simple spillover model". Keep it there.
2. **Open with the Part I divider.** Use the skeleton's
   `.section-divider .divider-carolina` slide with
   `data-footer-part="Part I · SpatialCRT: Spillover"` and
   `data-menu-title="Part I: SpatialCRT: Spillover"`.
3. **Footer labels and menu titles.**
   - Prefix every section label with the part: `data-footer-part="Methods"` becomes
     `"Part I · Methods"`, and the same for Background, Simulation Results, Application
     and Discussion.
   - Give every menu title an `I:` prefix, as the skeleton does (for example
     `data-menu-title="I: Model"`); author approved, 2026-10-28.
4. **Keep, rewrite or drop each slide** by its tag in `OUTLINE.md`:
   - **both:** keep as is;
   - **prelim:** keep (none at present);
   - **conference:** drop (title, Outline, Thank you).
5. **Background sets up the whole prelim.** Part I comes first, so its Background is
   the prelim's shared motivation: trials that randomize places, spillover (Ghana bed
   nets, Kenya deworming), spatial dependence, and the gap between analysis-stage and
   design-stage methods. Then "The question" states Part I's own aim. Part II builds on
   these slides and does not repeat them; its Background keeps only what is new
   (heterogeneous incidence, SUD, the pilot). Part I's model slide stays in full. Its
   notation (β intervention effect, ψ spillover, ρ spatial dependence, cluster-level
   outcome with binary rook spillover) deliberately differs from Part II's (τ, γ, β for
   incidence), and each part defines its own. Add one closing sentence to the last
   Discussion slide's script pointing to Part II (larger grids, heterogeneous incidence,
   queen neighbors and more designs).
6. **Backup.** Move the backup slides into the first of the three backup sections,
   under the skeleton's `Backup · Part I` divider
   (`data-footer-part="Backup · Part I · SpatialCRT: Spillover"`). They already carry
   the `.backup-slide` class and the "Backup ·" title prefix; change their menu titles
   from `B: …` to `BI: …` and their footer from `Backup` to the divider's label.
7. **Theme and figures.**
   - The three theme files are identical to Part II's copies, so Parts I and II look
     the same. Keep them unchanged.
   - Merge `theme/deck.css` into the prelim theme. Its classes are all `p1-` prefixed,
     so they do not clash with Part II's unprefixed classes (`.card`, `.glossary`,
     `.take-home` and so on). The one shared rule (column widths) is identical in both.
   - Copy `figures/` into `part-1/figures/` and update the image paths. The deck's
     numbers come from `tools/load_results.R`, which reads this repository's
     `results/` and `review/`: either keep the setup chunk with paths pointing back
     here, or copy `bss_rank_among_srs.csv` plus the three corrected tables and adapt
     the loader. Do not retype the numbers.
8. **Timing.** About 20 minutes per part, about 60 in total; Parts I and II may run a
   little longer while Part III is still brief. Without the standalone frame Part I is
   20 minutes by the script's markers. Re-time the notes after any cuts.
